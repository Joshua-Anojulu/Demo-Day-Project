About

How soccer resonates with me

Some of my earliest memories are of a soccer ball at my feet and my dad showing me how to strike it. We'd play in the yard until the light gave out, and somewhere in those evenings the game stopped being something I did and became part of who I am. Soccer is where I made my closest friends, where my family grew closer, and where I learned that a hard day could disappear the moment I stepped onto the field. It has never just been a sport to me. It's the thing that shaped me.

Cristiano Ronaldo

Widely regarded as one of the greatest of all time, known for his incredible physique, speed, and dribbling. He has won the UEFA European Championship and been awarded five Ballon d'Or titles.

Lionel Messi
