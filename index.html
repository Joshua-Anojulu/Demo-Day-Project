About

How soccer resonates with me

Ever since I was young, soccer has been my favorite sport. My dad played too, so he would train me and we would play together. Soccer was a significant and influential part of my childhood: it helped me make many friends, brought my family closer, kept me in great shape, and supported my mental health. Those are the reasons I believe soccer is the greatest sport ever created.
